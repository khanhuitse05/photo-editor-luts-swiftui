## Description (max 4000 chars)

```
SnapTune is a fast photo editor for beautiful color. Apply one-tap LUT filters, fine-tune your image with 13 pro adjustment tools, and save your favorite looks as reusable recipes.

Create cinematic, vintage, moody, bright, or custom styles in seconds. Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, white balance, tone, highlights, shadows, HSL, fade, sharpen, clarity, vignette, and blur. Crop, straighten, and refine perspective when your shot needs a cleaner frame.

Editing a set of photos? Use batch editing to apply the same look across multiple images at once. Mark favorite LUTs for quick access, return to recent edits, and export high-quality photos when you're done.

SnapTune is simple, native, and private. No account, no sign-up, and all editing stays on your device.
```
